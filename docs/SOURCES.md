## OpenAI Luna Reserve

Verified: **2026-09-05**.

Official source: [Luna Reserve in Codex and ChatGPT Work](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001499-luna-reserve-in-codex-and-chatgpt-work).

The article establishes that Luna Reserve is available only to selected accounts; selected personal Plus and Pro accounts are described, while Business and Enterprise workspaces are excluded; availability depends on account and supported app version; it provides additional GPT-5.6 Luna usage after regular usage is exhausted; regular and reserve allowances are separate; reserve is finite and not unlimited; it is not API credit; it does not restore regular usage or unlock the most capable models; and reaching a regular limit does not guarantee access.

AVF treats those as current provider facts, not core assumptions. Entitlement, remaining allowance, reset timing, effective model and transition cause remain runtime/account evidence. AVF does not assume universal eligibility, automatic detection, machine-readable quota percentages, or that an effective Luna model proves Reserve activation. Operational reports may be useful for investigation but are non-authoritative and are not used as framework facts.
